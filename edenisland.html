About the project

Lead a community.
Survive the change.

Eden Island Frontier is a 3D strategy and resource-management game about guiding a small community toward a sustainable future. Players gather resources through active minigames, construct and upgrade buildings, and make decisions that affect the community’s ability to survive changing weather and natural disasters.

Across five waves, players balance immediate resource needs against long-term environmental consequences. Sustainable upgrades can reduce future risk, while short-term gains may intensify later hazards. The project uses its city-building systems to make the effects of climate change tangible through play.
