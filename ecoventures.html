EcoVentures

About the project

Build a startup.
Waste less.

EcoVentures is a single-player arcade simulation–trivia game for Android, built in Unity with an original 3D art style. Set across FEU Institute of Technology campus environments, it follows a student startup founder building a successful and sustainable business.

Players collect materials through arcade-style challenges, craft products, and sell them to progress. The Zero Waste Infinite Solutions (ZWIS) initiative is woven into the game, encouraging responsible resource management and waste reduction across three increasingly demanding stages.
