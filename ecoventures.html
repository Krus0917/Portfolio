About the project

Make products.
Change habits.

EcoVentures is a single-player arcade simulation–trivia game for Android, built in Unity with an original 3D art style. Set across FEU Institute of Technology campus environments, it follows a student startup founder building a sustainable business from the ground up.

Players collect materials through arcade challenges, process them into products, and sell to customers before time and patience run out. Across three increasingly demanding stages, the game combines inventory management, crafting, customer behavior, trivia, and sales targets with the Zero Waste Infinite Solutions (ZWIS) initiative.

As a 3D Artist, I contributed to refining the game’s asset presentation and visual identity alongside the team’s gameplay and UI development.
